# Probity film storyboard

One settled still per motion scene (frames from the rendered `MotionDraft`; regenerate with `npx remotion still src/index.ts MotionDraft ../docs/video/storyboard/Mxx.png --frame=<n>` from `video/`). The film is one continuous world: a single glyph river on a `#0c0c0b` ground that morphs between layouts and is never cut. Recordings (R01..R13) wipe in and out over the river in a 1400 px hairline column; the river keeps flowing at both sides.

World vocabulary (all from `motion.md`): perspective 35 degree lens 1.2 u above the plane for river beats, orthographic isometric (30 degree elevation) for diagram beats, one move per shot on `bezier(.65,0,.35,1)`, glyph tiers `.` `:` `$` `$` on the stone ramp, red `x` only at the catch (at most 12 cells), step accents (cobalt, teal, violet, red) only on the agent planes. No bloom, glow, particles, shake or whip pans.
